## Clean Your TV launcher

The Clean Your TV launcher project is maintained separately at
https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv and is MIT licensed. TV APK Installer does not bundle
the launcher APK in its own package; the user selects an original Clean Your TV APK, from which
the Launcher and Curtain modules are extracted and installed separately on the Android TV.
